How GemDen reaches your phone.

Set up a calendar account. Then select your personal GemDen calendar when creating an appointment.

iPhoneAndroidDevice access
iPhone

Right in Settings.

  1. Create separate device access below.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Add Other Account → CalDAV Account. The names may differ slightly depending on your iOS version.
  3. Enter the server address, device username and device key shown here. The key is only shown once.
  4. Enable the calendar and select it in your calendar app.

Android

Sync in the background.

  1. Install a CalDAV sync adapter such as DAVx⁵ and use a compatible calendar app. DAVx⁵ may cost money depending on where you get it.
  2. Create device access here. In DAVx⁵, add an account using the server address and the corresponding device credentials.
  3. Allow calendar access and enable sync for your personal GemDen calendar.
  4. Show it in your calendar app. In Google Calendar, you may need to go to Settings → Manage accounts and enable the DAVx⁵ account.

Good to know

Your calendar stays yours.

Which appointments are synced?

Only entries in the personal GemDen calendar you deliberately connect. Other iCloud, Google or local calendars are not imported automatically. Select the GemDen calendar when creating new appointments.

Who can see my personal appointments?

Your personal calendar stays private in GemDen. Connecting your phone does not publish appointments in communities or on your profile. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone and its calendar can see the appointments stored there.

What about ThreeDen, Çiçek and shared meetups?

Your own confirmed ThreeDen and Çiçek requests may appear in your personal calendar. Changes to these arrangements are made in GemDen. Moving an entry in your calendar app does not replace confirmation. Public community calendars are a separate area; a calendar subscription usually only allows reading.

Are changes visible everywhere immediately?

Sync depends on your internet connection and your phone's refresh interval. If needed, refresh manually in your calendar app or sync adapter. If the calendar service is temporarily unavailable, syncing resumes later; the website calendars remain usable independently.

How are recurring events and reminders handled?

Recurring events, exceptions, all-day events and reminders are intended to be retained in your calendar app. Edit recurring and all-day events there. Very complex or unrecognised recurring events may appear with a notice on the website to keep the page usable.
