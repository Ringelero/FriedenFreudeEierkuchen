Friendship that makes things possible

A dynasty full of Omnikünstler.

The Rubybubys are the dynasty founded by Julius Rubybubius, growing out of his circle of friends. We share a wish to make the world a little better. A community of Omnikünstler: we make, repair, organise, care for one another and give creativity room to grow. Many worlds of our own – and the joy of making things possible together.
