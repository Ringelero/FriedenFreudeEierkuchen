How neighbourhoods, dynasties and permissions fit together

A neighbourhood connects everyday life in one place. A dynasty connects people across neighbourhood boundaries, too. You can belong to several communities.

Neighbourhoods and dynasties can develop their own ways of working and their own culture. Fundamental rights, data protection and shared technical interfaces remain separate. Blob and the website do not invent cultural names or rituals.

Still to be decided: The history, rituals, ranks and language of mastery of the Rubybubys will be decided later by the people authorised to do so.
