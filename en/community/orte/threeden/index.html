Three rooms. Many possibilities.

Which den draws you in?

Red for getting immersed. Green for making things together. Bright for enjoying the atmosphere of the neighbourhood.

Film & relaxation

The Red Room.

A media room for getting immersed and relaxing. The home cinema and string lights make it cosy, and the massage chair invites you to unwind. There is a bed up on the loft level.

  • Home cinema
  • String lights
  • Massage chair
  • Bed on the loft level

Games & crafts

The Green Room.

A little jungle with a large table for games and crafts. Plenty of tools and materials give ideas room to take shape. This is where we make things together.

  • Plant jungle
  • Large table
  • Games
  • Tools & materials

A window onto the neighbourhood

The Bright Room.

Comfortably furnished and open to the street: a rug, deck chairs or a hammock make this a place to enjoy the neighbourhood atmosphere. Somewhere to linger and feel part of it.

  • Open window
  • Rug
  • Deck chairs
  • Hammock
