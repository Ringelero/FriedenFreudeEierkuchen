How to use it

How to manage the entire account lifecycle.

Account access, member identity and additional permissions remain separate decisions. This allows each step to be checked individually and reversed if necessary.

  1. 1

    Invite

    Enter a name and email address and send the invitation. Supabase creates the account with a private profile draft and sends the link.

  2. 2

    Confirm member ID

    For the new account, check the suggested MEM-*-ID and assign it permanently. This ID remains stable.

  3. 3

    Accept invitation

    The member opens the email link, arrives in their personal account and sets their own password with at least twelve characters.

  4. 4

    Manage the account later

    The display name can be corrected with a reason, and a new one-time link can be sent if the previous link has expired. Disabling an account blocks sign-in and database permissions; reactivating it restores both.

  5. 5

    Optional neighbourhood permission

    Only if separately authorised: select a neighbourhood, document the reason and grant the limited permission. It can later be revoked with a new reason.

  6. 6

    Review history

    At the bottom of the workspace, you'll see the time, the management account that acted, the member concerned, the decision and the reason.

If an invitation link expires: The member opens their account, enters the same email address and requests a new one-time link there. There's no need to create a second account.
