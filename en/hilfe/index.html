Good to know.

How do I report a problem or an idea?

Choose “Write a ticket”, give your request a short title and describe what happens and what you would like. You can report problems, improvements, ideas and wishes. Please do not enter passwords.

Links on the pages include only the page path as context. Your text is sent only when you select “Send ticket”.

Where can I see replies to my ticket?

Find the status and replies under “My Tickets”. With an account, your tickets belong to your account. Guest tickets remain accessible in the same browser; keep its saved website data. Replies are not automatically sent by email.

Why is an overview still empty?

An empty calendar or list of offers initially means there are no visible entries for your selection yet. No meet-ups, availability or participating businesses are invented. You can change your filters or contribute a request of your own.

Does a request count as a confirmed arrangement?

No. Saying you might join a dog walk is non-binding. You explicitly discuss and confirm care arrangements and calendar requests yourselves. Profiles and self-reported information do not replace checking for yourselves whether an offer is right for you.

How does Blob help me?

Blob shows suitable paths and explains existing opportunities. It opens only when you select it. Personal account suggestions are optional; you can switch them off again in the suggestion settings. Help and Tickets are also available in the footer without Blob.
