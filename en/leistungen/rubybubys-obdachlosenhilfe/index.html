Homelessness support

Thinking together about what really helps.

We want to support people experiencing homelessness. Here we are collecting initial suggestions to develop further with the people in our neighbourhood.

The following ideas have not yet been implemented. We will agree specific activities, dates and partnerships together first.

Explore the first ideas

Ideas to develop further

Our first ideas.

These are possible starting points. What they become will depend on people's wishes and what we can reliably do together.

01 · Listen and share time

Make time for regular conversations as equals, when they are wanted. Find out together whether company, someone to accompany them or something else would be helpful right now.

02 · Ask what is needed, collect with purpose

Ask what is actually needed before starting a collection. For example, obtain suitable clothing, hygiene products or everyday items, and agree the handover in advance.

03 · Offer practical everyday support

Help with a specific task when asked: look up information, repair something or accompany someone to an agreed appointment. Any support is arranged directly with the person concerned.

04 · Share a meal

Consider a shared meal or a small cooking evening. First establish interest, find a suitable place, arrange ingredients and agree who can reliably take responsibility.

05 · Bring helpful information together

Collect easy-to-find information about existing support services, with original sources and dates. We want to find out together with local people which information is needed.

06 · Work together locally

Get to know existing initiatives and ask where support would be useful. Joint activities could develop once interest, tasks and responsibilities have been agreed together.

From ideas to next steps.

Next, we will choose an initial idea and find out who would like to help develop it. Further suggestions can be submitted as tickets.
