Community & your own ideasGet involved togetherStart something that everyone can benefit from.›

People with and without a home should shape this community together. We will begin with what the participants themselves want to try.

  • Cook, collect and share together
  • Make repairs, create art and share your own stories
  • Organise voluntary errands and mutual support

Small paid tasks, a shared kitchen and private visits or places to stay are further ideas. We will work out how they can function well with the people involved.

Our own activities and a private community circle are in preparation. The Rubybubys calendar shows community events that have already been published.
