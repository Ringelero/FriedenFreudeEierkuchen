Spend time together first

For Heart & Hound, we suggest meeting with the familiar person present. Show a suitable quiet route and explain which encounters your dog enjoys. The new person first takes on a manageable part while you remain reachable. An introduction is not an automatic agreement to several days of care.

Include this in the handover

  • Food, suitable rewards, resting place and familiar routines.
  • Equipment, recall word, off-lead agreements and known difficulties.
  • Contact availability, veterinary contact and agreed emergency steps.
  • Dates, tasks, possible costs and who confirms a change.

Hand over medicines only with the specific prescribed plan, including dose and administration. The carer should be able to demonstrate necessary actions beforehand.

Review a short trial

Our suggested feedback: could your dog rest, eat and toilet? Which encounter or touch was difficult? Shorten or change the next arrangement if the trial was too much. Discuss agreements and private contact details in the care area.
