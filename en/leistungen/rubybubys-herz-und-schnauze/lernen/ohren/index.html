Check ears and clean when needed

Distinguish routine checks from problems needing treatment.

A check does not automatically mean cleaning

Check smell, skin and visible wax while your dog participates comfortably. A little light-brown wax can be normal. With redness, pain, unusual smell, heavy discharge or frequent head shaking, have the ears examined before cleaning.

Read more: PDSA · Ear care · PDSA · Grooming

When cleaning has been recommended

Ask for a suitable dog ear cleaner and a demonstration. Have soft cotton wool or gauze ready. Gently lift the ear flap, apply cleaner as demonstrated and directed by the product and massage only while comfortable. Allow your dog to shake their head afterwards.

Read more: PDSA · Ear care

Wipe only the visible accessible area

Gently wipe loosened debris from the outside. Do not put cotton buds, fingers or bottle nozzles deep into the canal. Use prescribed drops and cleaner according to the individual plan, not a universal daily cleaning routine. Stop immediately if it hurts.

Read more: PDSA · Ear care · PDSA · Grooming

Helpful preparation

Our home practice suggestion: begin with lifting the ear flap and pausing afterwards. That is a complete small session before adding liquid or other actions.
