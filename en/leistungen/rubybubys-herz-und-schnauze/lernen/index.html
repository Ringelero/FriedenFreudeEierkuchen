Our approach, sources and images

We support clear feedback, choice and reward-based learning. Krishnamurti inspires our outlook; practical actions and exercises draw on the expert sources linked with each topic.

GemDen writes these explanations. We thank the expert providers for sharing their knowledge. Articles about care and health changes do not replace an individual examination.

The woodland illustrations were created for GemDen using image generation. The small light animation is our own design. These do not demonstrate medical procedures. External videos remain with their original providers and load only after you choose to load them.
