Puppies & everyday life

Build toilet habits without scolding

Notice the right moment, reward outside and understand accidents.

In this guide

Offer the next opportunity early

After sleeping, eating, drinking and play, take your dog to a suitable toilet spot early. Stay quietly nearby. Reward outside as soon as they finish; waiting until you are indoors makes the connection much less clear.

Read more: Dogs Trust · House training

If nothing happens outside

Return calmly and keep watching your dog. Floor sniffing, circling and preparing to squat may signal the next opportunity. An exciting walk does not necessarily replace a quiet toilet break.

Read more: Dogs Trust · House training

After an accident

Scolding or rubbing their nose in it does not help. Clean with an appropriate pet-safe enzymatic cleaner and adjust the next opportunity. Keep fresh water available. Sudden new accidents, pain or major changes in drinking need veterinary advice.

Read more: Dogs Trust · Toileting indoors

A pattern rather than blame

Our example note: “It happens right after waking.” Have lead and shoes ready before the next nap. Change the routine before expecting your dog to wait longer.
