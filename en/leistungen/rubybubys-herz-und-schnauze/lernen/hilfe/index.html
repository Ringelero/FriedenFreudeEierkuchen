When an article is not enough

Consider health and behaviour together

Sudden new defensiveness, restlessness or difficulty with touch need medical consideration too. Describe the onset and changes. For acute severe pain, injury or other urgent symptoms, contact a vet or local emergency service directly.

Read more: Dogs Trust · Reactive dogs

Questions to ask a behaviour professional

  • How do you assess history, and when do you involve the vet?
  • Which specific methods and tools do you use?
  • How do we recognise overwhelm, and how do we stop an exercise?
  • What education, ongoing training and experience do you have with this issue?

Read more: AVSAB · Humane training & choosing a trainer

You can ask questions and stop

Clear explanations, choice and an understandable plan belong together. Promises of an instant cure or methods relying on fear, pain and intimidation do not fit our approach. A title or logo alone does not explain the actual method.

Read more: AVSAB · Humane training & choosing a trainer

A short preparation for the appointment

Our suggestion: bring a description of a typical situation, changes so far and your most important everyday question. A safely obtained recording may help; there is no need to provoke a conflict.
