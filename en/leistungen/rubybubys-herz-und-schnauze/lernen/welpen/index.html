Make an experience manageable

For an initial experience of a busy street, watch from farther away. What matters is whether your puppy can still explore and look away again. If they hide or freeze, increase distance and end the outing if needed. The number of people or sounds ticked off is not a measure of success.

Read more: Dogs Trust · Puppy socialisation

Create security at home first

Set up a quiet sleeping place and retreat, remove accessible cables and other hazards and limit early visitors. Touch and introductions can wait when your puppy withdraws. Regular opportunities for toileting, eating and sleeping make the day predictable.

Read more: Dogs Trust · Settling into a new home

What to discuss with your vet

Suitable outdoor contact before and between vaccinations depends on individual protection and local infection risk. Agree a safe plan for early experiences. A general age figure is neither a blanket ban on experiences nor permission for every dog park.

Read more: Dogs Trust · Puppy socialisation

Our suggested note

“Watched bicycles from the entrance; use this distance again next time.” A calm repeat is often a better next step than an immediately bigger outing.
