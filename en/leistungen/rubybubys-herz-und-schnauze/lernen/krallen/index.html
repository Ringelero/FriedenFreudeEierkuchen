Care & hygiene

Check nails and trim them safely

Know the tools, the quick and the limits with dark nails.

Before the first cut

Have someone demonstrate the appropriate length and cut on your own dog. Prepare dog nail clippers, good lighting and rewards. Use a non-slip surface and support the paw comfortably. Introduce touch and the tool before clipping.

Read more: PDSA · Clipping dog nails · AKC · Dog grooming

The individual action

  1. On light nails, locate the pink quick. It contains nerves and must not be cut.
  2. Trim only the free tip, leaving a clear margin from the quick. Do not remove a large length at once.
  3. Reward afterwards and assess whether another nail is appropriate now. One nail may be enough today.

Read more: PDSA · Clipping dog nails

Dark nails and difficulties

If you cannot identify the quick, do not guess a cutting line from a picture: ask for a demonstration or professional trim. Remember dewclaws during checks. Stop if bleeding occurs, apply gentle pressure with clean gauze and contact your vet for persistent bleeding, injury or significant pain.

Read more: PDSA · Clipping dog nails · AKC · Dog grooming

Our suggested next practice step

If your dog withdraws the paw at the first touch, practise that contact without clippers first. Clipping faster does not solve the underlying difficulty.
