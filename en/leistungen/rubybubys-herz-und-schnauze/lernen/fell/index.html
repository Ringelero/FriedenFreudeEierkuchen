Brush the coat and bathe when appropriate

What does this coat need?

Short, long and curly coats and undercoats need different tools. Ask for a suitable brush and demonstration. During grooming, also check the skin and places where harness contact or movement encourages knots.

Read more: PDSA · Grooming

Keep brushing manageable

Start on a comfortably handled area with a few gentle strokes in the direction of the coat. Do not pull through painful knots. Matting close to the skin needs professional help; never slide scissors blindly under a mat. A shorter manageable coat may suit your dog better than a daily struggle to preserve length.

Read more: PDSA · Grooming

Bathe as needed

For light dirt, lukewarm water may be enough. When shampoo is needed, use a mild dog product, a non-slip surface and thorough rinsing. Keep water and shampoo away from eyes, nose and ears. Frequency and special skin care depend on coat, dirt and veterinary advice.

Read more: Deutscher Tierschutzbund · Fellpflege (Seite 12)

One area at a time

Our suggestion: divide brushing into clear body areas and pause after each. This helps identify a difficult spot before spending longer on it.
