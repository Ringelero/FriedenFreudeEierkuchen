The idea behind Herz und SchnauzeA service by the Rubybubys+

Animals should see familiar people more often and have more places to live their lives. People can share responsibility. We are starting with dogs and their people; other pets and hosts can join later.

Meeting places, walking windows and private arrangements are available. The host network is still growing.
