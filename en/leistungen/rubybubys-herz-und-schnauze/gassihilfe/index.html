Meeting builds trust.

  1. Walk together first.Take your time to see whether you feel comfortable with each other.
  2. Express interest privately.Only the person you contact can see your message. Your private conversation opens only after they accept.
  3. Arrange a walk.Discuss the dates and times, handover, contact arrangements and what the dog needs. An accepted request is not yet a commitment to provide care.

After a request has been accepted, you can go to My contacts & care arrangements to find each other again, record exact dates and times, and both confirm them. Listings are posted by the people themselves; they do not mean that the Rubybubys have checked or recommended them.
