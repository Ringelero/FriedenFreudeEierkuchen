Start small

What could work at your place?

A café, hostel or shop each has different possibilities. We're looking for an arrangement that suits the place, its people and the animals.

01

Feeling welcome

A visit with your own animal, with clear information about areas, times and conditions.

02

Finding calm

A suitable place to rest, with space and somewhere to retreat. Together, we'll check whether and how this can work on site.

03

Growing familiar

Regular encounters or a small gathering, if it suits everyone involved.

An invitation to visit does not imply any care arrangements. If an animal is to stay without their familiar person, this needs its own explicit agreement.

From idea to place

Get to know each other first.
Then open up together.

We're starting with dogs. Other pets may join later, with their own needs taken into account.

  1. The first conversationIntroduce the idea, explore interest and listen: what does the place itself want?
  2. Agree on the arrangementsWhich animals, which areas, which times? Discuss quiet spaces, contact arrangements, responsibilities and any costs.
  3. Approve the listing togetherAgree on the description, contact details and conditions with the business. Only then can a confirmed host appear on the website and map.
