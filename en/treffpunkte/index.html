Berlin places · sources & coverage

The Berlin selection now includes places in all twelve boroughs. It is not yet a complete directory of every park and walking route. A missing place says nothing about whether dogs are allowed.

Designated off-lead access applies only to the named area. No off-lead access does not mean dogs are prohibited. Follow signs on site; area markers are not verified entrances.

  • Berlin Open Data · dog exercise areas · DL-DE-Zero 2.0
  • Berliner Forsten · forest dog exercise areas
  • Senate response · dog exercise areas in Berlin

Additional borough sources are linked directly under each place. Thank you to the publishing authorities. Sources and data dates are part of the place descriptions.
