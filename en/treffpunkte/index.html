Berlin places · sources & coverage

Public parks and designated off-leash areas across all twelve boroughs. The growing selection is not a complete directory. General park information does not mean dogs may roam freely; unverified places have no walking slots.

Designated off-lead access applies only to the named area. No off-lead access does not mean dogs are prohibited. Follow signs on site; area markers are not verified entrances.
